# RakFort Printer Troubleshooting

## How printing works at RakFort
RakFort uses **secure follow-me printing**. Send your job to the **"RakFort-Print"** queue, then tap your staff badge on any office printer to release it. Jobs that are not released are deleted after **24 hours**.

Printers are named by floor, for example **RKF-FL2-PRN01** (floor 2, printer 1).

## Printer shows "Offline"
1. Check the printer's screen for errors (paper jam, empty tray, open door).
2. On your PC, open **Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Printers & scanners** and make sure **RakFort-Print** is your selected printer.
3. Remove and re-add the RakFort-Print queue from the **Company Portal** app.
4. If several people on your floor are affected, raise a P2 ticket - it is likely a printer or network fault.

## Toner, paper and physical damage
Toner, paper and physical printer faults are handled by the **Facilities team on extension 2200**, not IT.

## Badge not recognised at the printer
Your badge must be registered for printing. Raise a ticket with the Service Desk and include your badge number.
