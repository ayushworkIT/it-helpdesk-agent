# RakFort Password and Account Policy

## Password requirements
- Minimum length: **14 characters**. Passphrases are encouraged (for example, four random words).
- Passwords do **not** expire on a schedule. You must change your password immediately if you suspect it has been exposed.
- Do not reuse your RakFort password on any other website or service.

## Account lockout
- Your account locks after **5 failed sign-in attempts**.
- Locked accounts **unlock automatically after 30 minutes**.
- If you need access sooner, contact the Service Desk on **extension 4357**.

## Self-service password reset
- Reset your password at **https://passwordreset.rakfort.example**
- You must have registered **Microsoft Authenticator** first. New starters register during IT onboarding.

## Security rules
- RakFort IT will **never** ask for your password - by phone, email or chat.
- If you have shared your password with anyone (including a chatbot), change it immediately and report it to **security@rakfort.example**.
