# RakFort IT Service Desk - Contacts and Priorities

## How to contact IT
- **Support portal (preferred):** https://support.rakfort.example
- **Phone:** extension **4357** (spells HELP)
- **Email:** servicedesk@rakfort.example
- **Hours:** Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00

## Ticket priorities
| Priority | Meaning | First response |
|----------|---------|----------------|
| P1 | Whole office or critical system down | 30 minutes |
| P2 | A team or several people affected | 2 hours |
| P3 | One person affected, a workaround exists | 1 working day |
| P4 | Requests and questions | 3 working days |

## Out of hours
For P1 outages outside business hours, call the **on-call IT engineer on extension 4300**. All other issues should be logged on the portal for the next working day.

## Security incidents
Report phishing emails, lost devices or suspected account compromise immediately to **security@rakfort.example** or extension **4357**, option 1.
