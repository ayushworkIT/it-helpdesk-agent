# RakFort VPN Guide

## Overview
RakFort uses **Cisco Secure Client** for remote access. You must connect to the VPN to reach shared drives, the intranet and internal apps when working outside the office.

## Connecting
1. Open **Cisco Secure Client** from the Start menu.
2. Enter the server address: **vpn.rakfort.example**
3. Click **Connect** and sign in with your RakFort email and password.
4. Approve the sign-in request in **Microsoft Authenticator** on your phone.
5. Wait for the status to show **Connected**.

## Common problems
- **"Login failed"**: check Caps Lock, and check whether your password has expired. Reset it at https://passwordreset.rakfort.example
- **"Connection attempt has timed out"**: some hotel and public Wi-Fi networks block VPN traffic. Try a mobile hotspot. On hotel Wi-Fi, first open a browser and accept the network's sign-in page.
- **No MFA prompt on your phone**: open Microsoft Authenticator manually and check that notifications are enabled.

## Rules
- Never install the VPN client on a personal or public computer.
- Disconnect from the VPN when you finish work.
